Features

Built for how Indians actually spend.

Multi-source ingest

Bank statements, UPI app exports, and credit cards — Vitta parses them all and deduplicates via UPI transaction ID.

Tag once, remember forever

Tag a person-name payee once. Every past and future payment to that name auto-categorizes. Your dictionary compounds.

Ask anything, in plain English

"How much did I spend on food last quarter?" — Vitta reads your data and answers with real numbers, not made-up ones.

Recurring detection

Vitta finds every subscription — including the ones you forgot about. Same merchant, similar amount, monthly cadence.

Multi-account handling

Two bank accounts, three UPI apps, one credit card — no problem. Self-transfers between your own accounts auto-excluded.

Bank-grade security

Encrypted at rest. Source PDFs deleted after parsing. DPDP-compliant. Row-level security on every table. Never resold.
