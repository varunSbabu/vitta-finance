How it works

Three steps. Five minutes.

02

Parse

Vitta reads narrations, VPAs, remarks, and MCCs. Multi-source dedup via UPI transaction ID means zero double-counting.

Features

Built for how Indians actually spend.

Multi-source ingest

Bank PDFs, GPay and PhonePe exports — Vitta parses them all and deduplicates via UPI transaction ID.

Tag once, remember forever

Tag a person-name payee once. Every past and future payment to that name auto-categorizes. Your dictionary compounds.

Ask anything, in plain English

"How much did I spend on food last quarter?" — Vitta reads your data and answers with real numbers, not made-up ones.

Debt & split tracking

Split expenses with friends, track who owes you and who you owe. Settle debts and watch your net spend update in real time.

Multi-account handling

Two bank accounts, three UPI apps — no problem. Self-transfers between your own accounts auto-excluded.

Per-user isolation

Every query is scoped to your user ID. Row-level isolation on every table. Your data is never shared, never resold.

Your data. Handled with care.

Financial data is sensitive. Vitta stores only parsed transaction data — isolated per user with row-level scoping. Every database query is bound to your user ID. No data is shared or sold.
